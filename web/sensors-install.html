Localserve

Android 8.0 or later

Download Android APK
  1. Open this page on Android, download the APK and open the downloaded file. Allow installation from your browser if Android asks.
  2. Open Localserve → Connection and enter the server address below. Home loads your remotes and published Tasks.
  3. Tap Edit on Home to choose and arrange your favorites. Interface sounds are optional in phone preferences.

Optional phone reporting: enable the Sensors plugin, open Sensors in the Android app, select sources and tap Start. Grant access only for your selected sources. Devices appear automatically in Sensors. Stop reporting from the app or its notification.

The remote requires your running server and Wi-Fi connection. Use an up-to-date Android System WebView. Location and named regions run during explicit streaming. Activity transitions require Google Play services. Install over the existing Localserve Sensors app to retain its device identity and settings.

Reports use the local network without pairing credentials. Sampling and reporting rates can differ; each report contains the latest raw sensor values. Sensor availability depends on the Android device and granted permissions.
