Sormus administrator

Sign in to manage this server. Published displays and remote controls keep their separate access.

This browser stays signed in for up to 12 hours. Use Sormus on your trusted PC or private network; sign-in does not encrypt its HTTP connection.

Forgotten password?

On the server PC, stop Sormus normally and run runtime\node.exe server/admin-access-cli.mjs --disable, then start it again. This switches sign-in off and revokes administrator sessions. Existing Tasks and devices are retained.
