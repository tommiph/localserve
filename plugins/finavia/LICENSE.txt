Finavia plugin provenance

Adapter and declaration: authored for Localserve using its existing supervised
HSL Trains/Simulator runtime lifecycle. No external Finavia client source was
copied. Historical endpoint and XML field names were inspected in the public
nokka200/FinaviaApi repository. The signed-in Finavia portal and a successful
HTTPS apigw.finavia.fi response confirmed the current gateway, header and XML.

Finavia flight data remains subject to Finavia API general and specific terms,
not an assumed Creative Commons license. General terms are available at
https://apiportal.finavia.fi/signup. Public Flights was an active subscription
in the user's signed-in portal; no new subscription or terms were accepted.
