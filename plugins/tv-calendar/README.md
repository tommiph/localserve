# TV Calendar

Follow television shows and keep a personal episode calendar using public TVmaze listings. No TVmaze account or API key is needed.

1. Enable TV Calendar in its Settings tab.
2. Open Calendar and select Discover shows. The finder opens with 18 starter picks across sci-fi, drama, comedy and thrillers. Suggestions are separate from your watchlist and do not create Tasks or Events. Their metadata is a dated TVmaze snapshot; following loads current episode listings.

   Choose a genre to narrow the starter picks, or search for a title. Search for a title, optionally filter by genre and status, check the network and premiere year, then choose Follow. Each click saves immediately and changes to Following; keep selecting shows from the same results. Close the finder when finished. Add show opens the same search; no ID is required.

   With the title empty, choose Starter picks or browse today’s worldwide streaming releases or the United States, United Kingdom or Finland broadcast schedule. Genre-only browsing covers that selected day/source, not the entire TVmaze catalogue. Title searches cover the full catalogue; genre and status filters narrow the returned matches.
3. Followed shows are enabled and refresh on the next check; Shows also offers Refresh show. Use Edit to rename or disable a followed show. Copies use the ordinary editor and start disabled.
4. Switch between Month, Agenda and Shows. Select an episode to open its details and mark it watched.
5. Create an Upcoming television ready-made Card to display your next episodes in Vistas or Controller.

Dates follow the original network/streaming release and display in the server timezone with its selected time format. They do not confirm that an episode is available on a local subscription. Unknown times remain unknown. Cached listings remain visible during a provider outage.

Tasks can use Episode airing with the show name, episode title, season, number, air time, network and episode link. Events run on the next active check after the reported time; initial reads, restarts and long pauses do not replay old episodes. No notifications or playback Tasks are created automatically.

The Tonight on television Card includes episodes airing from 16:00 today until 06:00 tomorrow in the server timezone. These are followed shows, not a regional channel guide.

Each show retains up to 50 aired,50 upcoming and 10 undated episodes; the full guide is linked on TVmaze. Watched history is local, with a 10,000-entry limit. Metadata refreshes every 6,12 or 24 hours, one due show per check. Search and watched changes are administrator operations.

Listings provided by [TVmaze](https://www.tvmaze.com), licensed under [CC BY-SA](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Saved watchlists and watched history are private installation data and excluded from source distributions.

## Daily watch guide

Calendar > For you shows four daily discovery suggestions plus upcoming unwatched episodes from followed shows. Follow saves a suggestion with one click. Followed shows are excluded from new daily suggestions. Select any preferred genres and the completed-series option in Settings. Suggestions can match any selected genre; All genres removes that filter. Starter metadata carries its checked date and is not a claim that a show airs today or is available on your subscriptions. The normal episode check updates the daily selection.

Create Card opens the ordinary editor with Daily TV suggestions selected. A Fullscreen version is also available. Tonight on television and Upcoming television retain their existing meanings and source selection. No new notifications or playback Tasks are created.

## Themed Cards

This plugin includes Sci-fi, Hacker, Rustic and LCARS ready-made designs. Choose a source in the Card editor, or use **Vistas → Create themed Vistas** for the complete collection and Holographic Command presentation. Disabled or missing sources remain explicit; these read-only designs never send commands. See [Themed Cards and TV decks](../../THEMED-VISTAS.md).
